Harvard Alumni in Tech is a 100% volunteer-led organization that has grown without external funding. Fundraising will help us expand our programs, strengthen our alumni network, and create more opportunities for our community to connect, collaborate, and grow.

**Volunteer Responsibilities:**

- Help develop and execute fundraising strategies.

- Identify and engage potential donors and supporters.

- Support donor outreach and relationship-building efforts.

- Collaborate on fundraising marketing and communications campaigns.

- Brainstorm creative fundraising initiatives to increase participation and donations.

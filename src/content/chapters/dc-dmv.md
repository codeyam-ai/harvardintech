There are a lot of Harvard alumni working in tech and policy around Washington, and they already talk to each other — the DMV WhatsApp group is active and worth joining today.

What the chapter does not have yet is anyone organising events. That is the only thing standing between this and a full chapter: not interest, not numbers, just someone willing to pick a date and a room. Policy-meets-tech happy hours are the obvious place to start, and there is an existing community here that would show up to them.

If you would like to help make that happen — as a lead, or just as someone who will help out — say so below.

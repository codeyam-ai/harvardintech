Harvard Alumni in Tech is the official Harvard alumni group for technology. We
work to further the development of technology by encouraging innovation, giving
Harvard alumni technologists the resources and networks they need, and promoting
technological activity throughout the Harvard community.

This blog collects writing from across our community. Our longform interviews and
essays — including the ongoing **Harvard Alumni in Tech AI Edition** and **Spotlight**
series — are published on our Medium publication:

- [Read on Medium →](https://medium.com/harvard-in-tech)
